Days Cash On Hand

This ratio calculates the days operating cash and cash equivalents on hand to fund annual cash expenditures but specifically related to very liquid assets. Operating cash and investments are those resources that do not carry donor restrictions on the assets and are available for the organization to spend without restrictions. This number is divided by the sum of expenses minus depreciation and amortization. To improve this ratio operating cash or investments must increase at a rate greater than the denominator (annual cash expenditures). Conversely keeping operating cash or investments the same but cutting annual cash expenditures will also have a positive impact on this ratio.

Days Expenses In Unrestricted Net Assets

This ratio is calculated by dividing the net assets without donor restrictions by the total expenses by day (total expenses/365 days).

Days Expenses In Unrestricted Net Assets Excluding PPE

This ratio is calculated by dividing the net assets without donor restrictions (less property, plant and equipment AND notes payable) by the total expenses by day (total expenses/365 days).

Total Coverage Ratio

This is calculated by dividing Total Assets by Total Liabilities. The ratio can be used as an indicator of the organization’s ability to service its debt and meet its financial obligations.

Contributions Trend

This is the total contributions with and without donor restrictions for each year

Contributions Without Donor Restrictions

This is showing contributions without donor restrictions for each year.

Contributions Trend

This is the percentage increase or decrease from the prior year to depict if giving is trending up or down over the years.

Annualized Investment Return

This ratio is the average amount of money an investment earns each year. It is calculated by dividing the current year investment income over the change in investment from the prior year.

Functional Expense % - Administrative

This ratio calculates the percentage of total expenses that are allocated to expenses directly tied to carrying out the organization’s mission.

Functional Expense % - Program

This ratio calculates the percentage of total expenses that are allocated to the management and operations of the organization.

Functional Expense % - Fundraising

his ratio calculates the percentage of total expenses that are allocated to expenses directly related to seeking and collecting funds from donors.

Cost of Contributions (Raise $1)

This ratio calculates the cost to raise one dollar. It is calculated by dividing the fundraising expenses by the sum of all contributions (with and without donor restrictions).
