CFI Ratio

The Overall ratio is the Composite Financial Index score, a comprehensive metric to assess overall financial health. It integrates four core financial ratios including the Primary Reserve Ratio, Viability Ratio, Return on Net Assets Ratio and Net Income Operations Ratio. This score shows the institution's ability to manage its resources in the short term, meet long-term obligations, generate returns on its assets and maintain operational effectiveness.

Primary Reserve Ratio

This ratio compares an institution's expendable resources to its annual operating (functional) expenses, indicating how long the institution could function without new revenue. It reflects the financial flexibility of the institution by showing the duration it can sustain operations using current reserves.

CFI Net Income - Operations Ratio

The Net Income Operations Ratio is a financial metric used to evaluate how effectively an organization manages its operational income relative to its operational expenses. This ratio is particularly significant for assessing the operational efficiency and sustainability of an institution. A positive ratio indicates that the organization is not only covering its operational costs but is also generating surplus revenues that can be reinvested into the institution or reserved for future needs. A negative ratio suggests that the organization is running at a loss indicating inefficiencies or challenges that might be a threat to long-term sustainability.

CFI Viability Ratio

The Viability Ratio measures an institution's ability to cover its long-term financial obligations using its available financial resources. A higher ratio indicates greater financial health and the ability to manage and repay debts effectively.
