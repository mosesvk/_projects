This calculator is for informational purposes only and is not intended to provide an exhaustive and completely accurate representation of what each individual’s tax savings may be. Further, this calculator has certain baked-in assumptions for ease of use. Those assumptions are: that each person itemizes their deductions on their personal federal income tax return; that they know what federal income tax rate they are subject to; and the Colorado child care contribution credit cap applies based on filing status (). For personalized tax guidance, each person should consult their own tax advisor.

The “State Tax Deduction Savings” amount applies Colorado’s income tax rate of 4.4% to your donation amount. This income tax rate is fixed by statute and is reflected in this calculator as of March 2025.

The “Colorado Child Care Contribution Credit” is 50% of the amount of a contribution made to a qualifying Colorado childcare charitable organization. This calculator caps the credit at $20,000 for individuals and $40,000 for married couples filing jointly.

The “Federal Tax Deduction Savings” amount is based upon the chosen Federal Tax Rate applied to the federally deductible contribution amount. The federally deductible contribution amount is the donation amount reduced by the amount of the Colorado Child Care Contribution Credit.
