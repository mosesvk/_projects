This calculator is for informational purposes only and is not intended to provide an exhaustive and completely accurate representation of what each individual’s tax savings may be. Further, this calculator has certain baked-in assumptions for ease of use. Those assumptions are: that each person itemizes their deductions on their personal federal income tax return; that they know what federal income tax rate they are subject to; and each user has already reached the $10,000 cap on the federal deduction of state and local taxes. For personalized tax guidance, each person should consult their own tax advisor.

The “State Tax Deduction Savings” amount is based upon Colorado’s income tax rate of 4.4%. This income tax rate is fixed by statute and is accurate as of November 2023.

The “Colorado Childcare Tax Credit Amount” is 50% of the amount of a contribution made to a qualifying Colorado childcare charitable organization. The Credit is currently capped at $100,000.

The “Federal Tax Deduction Savings” amount is based upon the chosen Federal Tax Rate applied to the federally deductible contribution amount. The federally deductible contribution amount is the Donation Amount reduced by the amount of the Childcare Contribution Tax Credit.
